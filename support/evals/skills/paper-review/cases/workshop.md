Pairs with the field profile matching the paper.

```yaml
venue_type: workshop

score_style: none                        # or a light 1–3

```

- Short form / free comments; often non-archival; page limit inherited from the
  parent conference; ethics/formatting norms inherited too.

## Assertions

1. Static mode: `review-area-chair` produces a **short organiser note** (agree /
   disagree / what would change the outcome) + accept/reject, and a **minimal**
   `review-B` (only `blocking` / `major`). No full merge, no meta-review essay.
2. `--mode loop`: step 7L is **skipped** (`response_routing: none`); the skill
   goes straight to the decision.
3. Deliverable A notes non-archival status if applicable.
4. The review is proportionate to a workshop — not a full conference review.
