# Fallback form — ML/CS conference (generic, NeurIPS/ICLR-shaped)

> **Fallback in use.** The real review form for this venue+year could not be
> found. This generic ML-conference form is a stand-in. Deliverable A must state
> this in bold.

`last_reviewed: 2026-09-06`

## Fields (in order)

| Field | Instructions |
|---|---|
| Summary | Neutral restatement of the paper's claimed contributions in your own words. |
| Strengths | Concrete, tied to specific parts of the paper. |
| Weaknesses | Discrete, taxonomy-tagged items, ordered by severity. |
| Questions | Answerable in a rebuttal; each would change your assessment. |
| Limitations | Has the paper adequately addressed its limitations and potential negative societal impact? |
| Additional feedback | Not part of the decision: presentation, minor points, suggestions. |
| Ethical concerns | Flag anything needing an ethics review. |

## Scores

| Score | Scale |
|---|---|
| Quality | 4 excellent · 3 good · 2 fair · 1 poor |
| Clarity | 4 excellent · 3 good · 2 fair · 1 poor |
| Significance | 4 excellent · 3 good · 2 fair · 1 poor |
| Originality | 4 excellent · 3 good · 2 fair · 1 poor |
| Overall | 6 Strong Accept · 5 Accept · 4 Borderline accept · 3 Borderline reject · 2 Reject · 1 Strong Reject |
| Confidence | 5 absolutely certain · 4 confident · 3 fairly confident · 2 willing to defend · 1 educated guess |

## Decision set

`accept (oral)` · `accept (spotlight)` · `accept (poster)` · `borderline` · `reject`

## Notes

- A lack of state-of-the-art results is not by itself grounds for rejection.
- Double-blind assumed: do not identify or speculate about the authors.
