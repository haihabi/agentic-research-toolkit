# Fallback form — journal referee report (generic, IEEE-Transactions / Nature-shaped)

> **Fallback in use.** The real reviewer instructions for this journal could not
> be found. This generic referee-report structure is a stand-in. Deliverable A
> must state this in bold.

`last_reviewed: 2026-09-06`

## Report to the authors (prose, numbered points)

1. **Summary of the manuscript** — what was done, what is claimed, in your words.
2. **Novelty and significance** — What is new relative to the literature? Is the
   advance sufficient for this journal? (For broad-scope journals: is it of
   interest beyond the immediate subfield?)
3. **Validity** — Are the conclusions fully supported by the data / derivations /
   measurements? Methodology sound? Controls and comparisons adequate?
4. **Statistics and reporting** — Are analyses appropriate and reported to a
   standard that allows assessment and reproduction? Error bars, n, tests,
   assumptions.
5. **Reproducibility and data/code availability** — Is there enough to reproduce
   the central result? Are data- and code-availability statements present and
   adequate?
6. **Major points** — numbered; each is a discrete taxonomy-tagged item.
7. **Minor points** — numbered; wording, figures, references.

## Confidential comments to the editor

- Overall assessment and recommendation.
- Any concern not appropriate to share verbatim with the authors (suspected
  misconduct, competing interests, priority disputes).
- Your expertise on this manuscript (high / medium / low).

## Recommendation (to the editor)

`accept` · `minor revision` · `major revision` · `reject & resubmit` · `reject`

## Required statements to check are present

Competing-interests declaration · funding disclosure · data availability
statement · code availability statement · ethics / IRB / consent where human or
animal subjects are involved · use-of-LLM disclosure where applicable.
