# Fallback form — IEEE conference (generic, ICASSP-shaped)

> **Fallback in use.** The real review form for this venue+year could not be
> found. This generic IEEE-conference form is a stand-in. Deliverable A must
> state this in bold.

`last_reviewed: 2026-09-06`

## Fields (in order)

| Field | Instructions |
|---|---|
| Summary of the paper | What is proposed and what is claimed. |
| Strengths | Specific and detailed. |
| Weaknesses | Discrete, taxonomy-tagged items, ordered by severity. |
| Detailed comments | Section-by-section technical comments to the authors. |
| Relation to prior work | Is the most relevant prior work (including prior IEEE work) cited and compared? |
| Presentation and English | Readability, figures, notation, length vs. page limit. |

## Scores

Use the full range; give the top or bottom score when it is deserved and can be
defended; reserve middle scores for genuinely middling papers.

| Score | Scale |
|---|---|
| Overall recommendation | 5 definite accept · 4 probable accept · 3 borderline · 2 probable reject · 1 definite reject |
| Novelty / originality | 5 high … 1 low |
| Technical correctness | 5 solid … 1 flawed |
| Relevance to the conference | 5 high … 1 low |
| Reviewer confidence | 4 expert · 3 knowledgeable · 2 some familiarity · 1 educated guess |

## Decision set

`accept` · `reject`  (IEEE conferences generally decide in one round; note in the
meta-review if the work would clearly succeed after a revision that the format
does not allow.)

## Notes

- Goal: accept papers that are technically sound and make an original and
  substantial contribution.
- Short, sketchy, or superficial reviews are not acceptable.
